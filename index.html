Stickman Uploader

A private publishing tool. Last updated 24 October 2026.

Stickman Uploader is a personal tool used by its owner, Muhammad Feman, to upload videos to and manage his own YouTube channels: Stick to Money and Giggle Grove.

It runs only on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos to the owner's channels, always as private first.
  • Sets titles, descriptions, thumbnails and channel branding on those channels.
  • Changes a video's visibility only after the owner approves it.
